About CampusHub

CampusHub is a modern student portal designed to simplify campus management and improve communication between students, teachers, and administrators.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make campus management simple, organised, and accessible. CampusHub reduces manual work by providing a digital platform where students and faculty can efficiently manage academic activities and records. The system supports essential functions such as attendance tracking, student information management, semester updates, and attendance calculation. By digitising these processes, CampusHub helps improve coordination and efficiency for both students and faculty at the Management Institute of Durgapur, making everyday academic administration faster, clearer, and more organised.

What We Do

Student Management

Store and manage student information securely.

Academic Records

Access student data, semester details, and academic records easily.

Department Control

Manage departments, semesters, and campus activities efficiently.

Secure System

Firebase authentication and secure database storage.
